Passion4Growth (Sofia) runs done-for-you B2B outreach for wholesalers, exporters, logistics and B2B service companies across CEE, DACH and Greece.
Weeks 1-2: we study your website, your current customers and the ones who said no; you approve the ideal-customer profile, who to avoid, and the target company list before anything is sent.
We build the list (directories, Google Maps, trade-fair exhibitor lists, databases), research each top account, write the offer and messages in the local language, and run email, LinkedIn and phone. WhatsApp or Viber only after someone replies. Each country's legal rules are checked first.
Sending runs from separate warmed-up domains, never your main one; LinkedIn and phone start in week 2-3, email after 2-3 weeks of warm-up. A person approves every reply before it goes out.
You get a weekly report: meetings, replies, and the honest reason when numbers are missing. First meetings usually come in 30-60 days; no number of meetings is guaranteed.
Price: EUR 500 one-off setup (domains, inboxes, tools) + EUR 1,500 a month, 3-month minimum.
