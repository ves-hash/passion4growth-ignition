# Buyer panel: Passion4Growth: revenue architecture for B2B companies in CEE (positioning, ICP and offer, outreach and partnership engine, deal flow and KPIs), with the P4G outreach skill pack as the outreach engine

**4 buy · 16 pass** (20% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 23, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- If the 15 sample companies are ones I'd actually want and the logistics reference tells me on a call what they paid per meeting and how many turned into deals, I'd sign. I'd sign faster if more of the money only became due once meetings had taken place.
- A real phone call with the logistics client who got 45 meetings, and nothing paid up front, so I only pay per qualified meeting that actually happens.
- A call with the past client from the closest niche, ideally an IT services or SaaS company selling into DACH, who tells me plainly that the outreach sounded like them and caused no complaints.
- Talking to a past client who is a manufacturer or exporter like us, not a logistics company, who tells me the meetings turned into real orders, plus 15 sample companies that are better than the ones my own team would have picked.
- Talking to a past SaaS or IT services client who got real DACH meetings that turned into pipeline, plus a sample list good enough that I don't already know half the names on it.
- If the 15 companies they send are buyers I actually recognise as right for my products, and I can talk to a past client who is a distributor like me and got real orders, not just meetings, out of it.
- Talking to a past SaaS or IT services client who actually closed deals in DACH from these meetings, plus a 15-company sample with names I don't already know and would really want to meet.
- If the 15 sample companies are real buyers I do not already know, and the past client in my niche tells me the meetings turned into orders, I would start the Sprint in Q1, timed to the market entry.
- Talking to the past client in a wholesale or distribution niche close to ours, plus our lawyer signing off on the GDPR pack, timed for when the board has confirmed the target country.
- Talking to a past client who runs an agency like mine and actually signed paying work from those meetings, or having the whole fee paid only per meeting with no upfront payment.
- If the 15 companies on the list are ones I really want and don't already know, and I can call a past client who is a wholesaler or distributor and hear that the meetings turned into actual orders.
- If the past client they put me in touch with was a manufacturer exporting into a new country like us, and said the meetings turned into real orders and not just polite calls, I'd book the Sprint when we start planning the market entry.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
