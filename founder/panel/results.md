**2 buy · 18 pass** (10% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 17, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## Why they pass

| reason | buyers | in their words |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| trust | 15 | "That's EUR 5,000 committed before I know if I get a single meeting, and you tell me straight out nothing is guaranteed and the first meeting might take two months. I paid an agency before and got zero meetings, so without a cost per meeting and a payback figure I'm not signing." (P001) · "My customers come from people who know me, from fairs and from calls I make myself. I don't believe a stranger sending cold emails in my name will bring a serious wholesale buyer, and EUR 5,000 for three months with no meetings promised is a lot of money for my size." (P002) |
| price | 2 | "EUR 5,000 minimum for three months with no meetings promised is what I'd pay a junior SDR plus Lemlist, and I've already seen I can scrape lists and send sequences myself for a fraction of that. Plus two weeks of profile approvals and handing over my customer list is exactly the homework my team doesn't have time for." (P004) · "EUR 6,500 locked in for three months with no meetings guaranteed is a lot for a firm like mine. I've run Lemlist and a freelancer myself, and I think I can get most of this done in-house for a fraction of that." (P020) |
| habit | 1 | "We're a SaaS company, so we already have people who know how to run Apollo and LinkedIn. EUR 5,000 minimum for 'no number of meetings is guaranteed' is something my partner would kill in thirty seconds when we could buy the tools and do it ourselves." (P016) |

## What would flip a no

- A month-one pilot with no 3-month minimum, or part of the fee tied to qualified meetings, plus two references from manufacturers or exporters my size who can show me their cost per meeting.
- A wholesaler I know and trust telling me personally that it brought them real new accounts without any complaints, or paying mostly per meeting that actually happens instead of a fixed monthly fee.
- Two or three logistics or 3PL firms like mine, ideally in the country I'm entering, that I could call myself and that got real meetings from this without complaints, plus a written legal opinion on GDPR for that country.
- A one-country pilot for the market we're entering next year, priced per qualified meeting or with part of the fee refunded if no meetings come in 60 days, and with the approval steps cut down to one short call.
- A wholesaler like mine, ideally someone I know or can call, telling me they got real orders from it, plus a short paid trial of one month without the 3-month lock-in.
- Pay per qualified meeting, or a one-month trial with no minimum and proof from a wholesaler like me in Bulgaria that got real orders, not just replies.
- Two or three wholesalers like us I can call myself, who tell me it brought real meetings in a new country without hurting their name, plus a written GDPR basis per country, timed for when we enter the new market.
- A cost per qualified meeting from real clients in wholesale, ideally in the country we're entering, plus part of the fee tied to meetings delivered or a way out after month one if there are none.
- References from two or three manufacturers or exporters my size with their actual cost per qualified meeting, plus a written GDPR basis (legitimate interest assessment) and a first month I can cancel if no meetings come.
- A paid pilot of one month with no minimum, or part of the fee tied to qualified meetings held, plus two references from SaaS or IT services founders in CEE I can actually call.
- Pay per qualified meeting, or a one-month paid pilot with no minimum, plus two or three references from SaaS or IT services companies selling into DACH that I can call myself.
- A reference call with a manufacturer or exporter like us in the CEE region who has run it for six months, plus a written GDPR / legitimate-interest assessment and a contract saying who is the data controller and who is liable.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
