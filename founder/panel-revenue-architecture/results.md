## What would flip a no

- A call with that logistics client so I can check the 45 meetings myself, plus a refund or free months if the sprint brings fewer than an agreed number of qualified meetings.
- Talking to that logistics client directly, plus a written cost-per-qualified-meeting target with part of the Run fee refunded if they miss it.
- Payment tied to results, like a big part of the fee only after qualified meetings happen, plus a talk with that logistics client to check the 45 meetings for myself.
- A reference call with a SaaS or IT services founder selling into DACH who used it without any complaints, plus my sign-off on every message before it goes out.
- A reference from a manufacturer in my own sector that I can call myself and that confirms the meetings turned into real orders, plus a pilot where they do almost all the work and the price depends on qualified meetings.
- A named SaaS or IT services reference in DACH that I can actually call, plus part of the sprint fee tied to the first qualified meetings (for example half refunded if no meetings come in by day 60).
- Talking to another distributor my size here who got real repeat orders from it, not just meetings, or paying mainly when a meeting turns into a customer.
- Part of the fee tied to results, like paying the Sprint balance only after the first qualified meetings, plus a reference I can call from a SaaS or IT services company selling into DACH.
- A written estimate of meetings per month for a wholesaler entering my target country, with part of the Run fee tied to qualified meetings and a clear cap on my team's hours.
- Speaking with a past client in wholesale or distribution who used it in another CEE country, and seeing in writing exactly who checks the GDPR side and that I approve every message before it goes out, so I can show my partner it is safe.
- A pay-for-results or partial-refund setup tied to qualified meetings, or the Sprint split so I pay a small amount first and the rest only after the first campaign books real meetings, plus a reference call with an agency client I can actually phone.
- A wholesaler or distributor in Bulgaria I can call who tells me how many real orders came from it, and paying part of the fee only when a meeting with an actual buyer happens.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
