## What would flip a no

- If the free list of 15 companies included names I recognise as real buyers I couldn't reach myself, and they gave me a reference I could call from a wholesale or distribution company. Then I'd have something to put in front of my partner.
- A wholesaler like me, not a logistics firm, who I can call and who tells me the meetings turned into real orders.
- The free list naming 3-4 buyers we genuinely want and can't reach ourselves, plus a reference from a CEE wholesaler my partner can call.
- If the 15 free target companies include real buyers in my niche that I didn't already know from trade fairs, and the first message in the local language is better than what my own people would write, I would run the pilot.
- If the free list of 15 has companies I didn't already know and would really want, and they let me talk to a manufacturing or export client who got real quote requests through the Relationship mode, I'd start the pilot before our next trade fair.
- If the 15 free target companies turn out to be ones I don't already know and that clearly fit, and they can show me a manufacturing or export client I can phone myself who got real quote requests, I would run the relationship-mode pilot.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
