## What would flip a no

- If the free list of 15 companies turns out to be companies I didn't already know and actually want, and I can call another wholesaler in Bulgaria who has used you, I would bring it to my partner.
- If the free list of 15 has companies I didn't already know and that really fit, and I can call another distributor my size in the region who used them and got real orders, not just meetings.
- A named manufacturing or export client in my kind of market who got meetings that turned into quotes, plus seeing that the free 15 companies are ones I couldn't have found myself in an afternoon on Apollo.
- A wholesaler or distributor like me, in the country I'm entering, who tells me on the phone that these meetings turned into real orders. Then I'd take the free 15 companies and probably the pilot.
- If the free list of 15 companies included two or three I already know are the right buyers, and they could show me a reference from a manufacturer or exporter like us that I can call myself.
- A phone call with a manufacturer or exporter of my size who used the pilot and can tell me how many of those meetings became real quotes or orders.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
