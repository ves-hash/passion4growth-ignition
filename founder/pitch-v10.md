Passion4Growth (Sofia) works only with logistics, wholesale and manufacturing companies in CEE that are entering a new country or need to replace lost volume: we get you meetings with buyers there, beyond referrals and trade fairs.
Step 1, free: after one 30-minute call, within 5 working days we show you 15 real target companies in your niche and market (the companies, not their contacts), why each one fits, and the first message we would send in the local language, plus a one-page summary for your partner or board. If it does not look right, you walk away.
Step 2, the 60-day Pilot, capped at EUR 2,800. You pay nothing up front. Your first 3 qualified meetings are free. The next 8 cost EUR 350 each, so the most you can ever pay in the pilot is EUR 2,800. Any meeting that turns out not to be a real buyer (wrong person, no need, no budget): mark it within 48 hours with the reason and it is free. After the pilot you decide whether to continue at EUR 350 per meeting, or stop.
Qualified means: a decision-maker (owner, buying, logistics or commercial lead) at a company you approved, in a call, a visit or a meeting at a trade fair, about buying from you. We sign this definition together.
You choose how we reach out. Volume mode: email from separate warmed-up domains plus LinkedIn and phone. Relationship mode: no mass email at all; only companies you approve one by one (at most 50), each with a researched, personal message signed by you, mostly on LinkedIn, by phone and at trade fairs, sent only after you approve it.
Going to a trade fair? We book meetings with target buyers at your stand before you travel, so the outreach leads to a handshake, not just an email.
Everything stays yours: at the end you keep the domains, the target lists, the messages and a written playbook, so your own team can carry on without us.
What we do in both modes: targeting, offer and outreach in the local language, with each country's legal rules checked and a GDPR pack (checklist, legitimate-interest assessment, a contract that says who controls the data). You approve the target list and every first message; a person approves every reply. Before each meeting you get a one-page brief; after it we send the follow-ups until you have a quote request or a clear no. Your time: about 3 hours in total, plus the meetings. Proof so far: a logistics client got 45 meetings in 2 months.
