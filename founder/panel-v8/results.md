These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- If the free list of 15 has real buyers I don't already know, and I can call that logistics client myself to hear how many of the meetings became orders, I'd put the pilot to my partner.
- If my own campaign using their free list gets nothing in a month, or they show me a manufacturer or exporter of our size who got real buyer meetings in a new country and I can call them.
- If the free list of 15 companies included two or three I didn't already know and that were clearly real buyers in our niche, plus a reference call with a wholesaler or distributor client (not just logistics) who got real quote requests.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
